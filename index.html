Straight answers

Questions I get asked

Do you work with businesses outside NDIS and aged care?

No. One sector only. That's the whole point. Your challenges are the brief, not a demographic I'm guessing at.

Am I locked into a contract?

No lock-in contracts. If the work isn't earning its place, you shouldn't be trapped paying for it.

Who actually does the work?

I create the strategy and execute the plan with my small team of specialists who kick ass at what they do. I always remain your point of contact and key strategist.

How fast will I see results?

Depends on the channel, and anyone who tells you otherwise is selling. Ads can put you in front of people searching this week. SEO is a long game. It builds visibility that keeps paying after you stop spending. Running both covers the short term and the long term.
