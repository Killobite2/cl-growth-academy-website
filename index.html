Straight answers

Questions we get asked

Do you work with businesses outside NDIS and aged care?

No. One sector only. That's the whole point — your challenges are the brief, not a demographic we're guessing at.

Am I locked into a contract?

No lock-in contracts. If the work isn't earning its place, you shouldn't be trapped paying for it.

Who actually does the work?

Chris. An approach designed for you, done by him — one point of contact, not a rotating account team reading from a deck.

How fast will I see results?

Depends on the channel, and anyone who tells you otherwise is selling. Ads can put you in front of people searching this week. SEO is a long game — it builds visibility that keeps paying after you stop spending. Most providers run both for that reason.

Will you use stock photos of smiling strangers?

Never. Real stories from your team build more trust than any stock photo ever will. We'll help you capture them on a phone — no production crew required.
