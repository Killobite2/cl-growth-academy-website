Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

This policy explains what personal information CL Growth Academy collects through this website, why, and what we do with it.

CL Growth Academy Pty Ltd (ABN 72 682 799 417) runs this website. In this policy, "we", "us" and "our" mean CL Growth Academy. We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988.

What we collect

When you fill in the contact form, we collect your name, email address, phone number and your message.

When you sign up to the newsletter, we collect your email address.

When you visit the site, our analytics and advertising tools collect information about your visit: the pages you view, how you arrived, the device and browser you use, and your approximate location based on your IP address. These tools use cookies and similar technology.

Please do not send us information about NDIS participants, clients or anyone's health or disability through the contact form. If we need that kind of detail to help you, we will talk about how to share it safely.

Why we collect it

  • To reply to your enquiry and talk about working together.
  • To send you the newsletter, if you signed up.
  • To understand how people use the site, so we can improve it.
  • To measure our advertising and show our ads to people likely to find them useful.

The tools we use

  • Web3Forms delivers contact form messages to our inbox.
  • beehiiv runs our newsletter and stores subscriber email addresses.
  • Google Tag Manager loads our analytics and advertising tools on the site.
  • Google Analytics measures how people use the site.
  • Meta Pixel (Facebook and Instagram) measures our ads and lets us show ads to people who have visited the site.

Some of these providers store information outside Australia, including in the United States. Each one handles the information under its own privacy policy.

Cookies and how to opt out

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. You can also:

  • opt out of Google Analytics with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on: https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout (opens in a new tab)
  • change how Google shows you ads at https://adssettings.google.com (opens in a new tab)
  • change how Meta uses information from other websites in your Facebook or Instagram settings, under Ad preferences.

Blocking cookies does not stop the site from working.

Who we share it with

We share your information only with the tools listed above and with members of our team who need it to reply to you. We do not sell personal information.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details while we are in contact with you and for a reasonable period afterwards. You can unsubscribe from the newsletter at any time using the link in every email, and we will stop sending it.

Your choices

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email chris@thegrowthacademy.com.au and we will respond within 30 days.

Complaints

If you have a concern about how we have handled your information, email chris@thegrowthacademy.com.au first and we will work with you to resolve it. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at https://www.oaic.gov.au (opens in a new tab)

Changes to this policy

We will update this page if what we collect or the tools we use change. The date at the top shows the latest version.
